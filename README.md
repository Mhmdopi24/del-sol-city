Del Sol City is an original open-world crime game that plays in a desktop browser from static files — no build step, no network requests. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car: steal and drive any vehicle, dodge or fight the police as your wanted level rises, and work through a five-mission story as Mara Vale, a driver-for-hire trying to buy her way out of the city. It has live traffic and pedestrians, a wanted system with police chases, combat, a HUD with a minimap, a start screen, and a save you can continue later.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome and play with keyboard and mouse (WASD to move and drive, E to enter or leave a car, mouse to look and shoot, Shift to sprint).
